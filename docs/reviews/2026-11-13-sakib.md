## Minor
### 🟡 Composite index coverage on tenant movements, [`InvMovement`](file:///home/sakib/Desktop/Projects%20Old/realbiz/realbiz_pro_server/src/database/models/inventory/inv-movement.model.ts)
**Problem**: The `inv_movements` table currently indexes `org_id`, `movement_no`, `movement_type`, and `movement_date` individually.
**Why it matters**: In high volume inventory environments with millions of records, querying the ledger by organization and date range (`WHERE org_id = ? AND movement_date BETWEEN ? AND ?`) will perform an index intersection rather than a direct compound scan.
**Suggested fix**: Add a composite index on `(org_id, movement_date DESC)` to optimize high throughput ledger and reporting queries.

### 🟡 Idempotency keys on disbursement payments, [`SettlementService`](file:///home/sakib/Desktop/Projects%20Old/realbiz/realbiz_pro_server/src/modules/lams/settlement/settlement.service.ts#L80-L105)
**Problem**: Recording installment disbursements relies on request reception without a unique client idempotency token.
**Why it matters**: If a network timeout occurs while the client is submitting a bank transfer disbursement, an automated retry could potentially record duplicate disbursements.
**Suggested fix**: Accept an optional `idempotency_key` or unique `reference_no` and enforce database level uniqueness scoped to `(org_id, reference_no)`.
